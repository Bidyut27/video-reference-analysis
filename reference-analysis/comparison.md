## What all three share (the "house formula")

1. **Wordless storytelling.** No narration or dialogue, so the videos travel across languages. The caption/title carries the premise as a question ("Did anyone else...?", "Who has been in this situation?"), which invites comments.
2. **Everyday local situation, exaggerated.** Bike rides, bus seats, traffic stops are universal and instantly understood.
3. **One clear protagonist with a flaw** (too small, overconfident, cocky).
4. **9:16, 24 fps, subject centred in the safe zone, hard cuts only.**
5. **Mixed loud and consistent** (−14 LUFS).
6. **Ends on a reaction or punchline frame**, with no outro or CTA card. This maximises loops.

## Weaknesses to improve on in original work

- **Video 1** has no punchline, and its 7 s shots are risky for cold audiences. Add a clear payoff and a mid-video hook.
- **Video 2** has an unchanged music bed at the reveal, so the twist lands softer than it could. Cut the music or add a stinger. The birth-year hook may also confuse non-local viewers. Make it legible (for example "Born 1999" / "Born 2008").
- **Video 3** has some cuts under 0.4 s that are hard to read, and the prop text is in local language only. Use icons plus one word and keep each shot ≥0.5 s unless it is a deliberate flash.
- **All three** run 20–37 s. Reaching **60–90 s** needs a stronger multi-beat structure (see the [blueprint](../production/blueprint.md)), not just slower pacing.
